Meet the family

Hi, we're Jimmy and Sara and our five kids.

The kids

Elliana (Ellie)

Our firstborn, and the one who showed us what a parent's heart can hold. Ellie is an artist, a writer, an anime fan, and a music lover who sees the world in color and stories.

Jacob

Our only son, outnumbered and unbothered. Jacob is a gamer, an athlete, a LEGO builder, and a filmmaker who turns an afternoon with friends into a short movie.

Amelia

Our middle, and our helper. Amelia cooks, sews, and crafts, and she gives her time volunteering at martial arts. Her friends know she'll always show up.

Katherine (KK)

Our fourth, with her nose in a book and a new game always brewing. KK invents worlds with her friends and is the self-appointed keeper of our cats.

Charlotte (Charlie)

Our youngest, and the bravest little shadow. Charlie and KK love their toys and dolls, and they follow their big sisters into every new adventure.

Together

Together they're regulars at our local public library and anything happening in our community. You'll find them watching science and maker videos with Dad, or helping Mom tend her succulent garden around the house. And there's always a business plan in the works: face painting, baked goods, handmade gifts, or the classic lemonade stand. If you see one, stop and buy a cup. You're investing in big dreams.
